### About the Speaker
**Dr. Subhash Kak** is an Indian-American computer scientist, Vedic scholar, and author. He is Regents Professor Emeritus of Computer Science at Oklahoma State University and an internationally recognized expert on ancient Indian science, astronomy, and philosophy.

### About the Presentation
This scholarly presentation explores the sophisticated mathematical traditions of ancient India, spanning the Sulba Sutras, astronomical calculation matrices, and the philosophical underpinning of infinite series and sacred geometry.
